05Recommendation and handover

If it were my call: Threshold.

1It is the only one with a person in it, and trust is about who is on the other side.
2The arch carries the weight of a bank or a courthouse; the dot keeps it at human scale.
3It fills an app icon, survives 16 px, and the dot can work alone as a badge or loading state.
Runner-up: Overlap, if you would rather the mark explain the product than welcome the person.
